## What does the site itself store?

Only your choice of light, dark or system theme, saved in your browser's
local storage so the next page uses it. No cookies are set by the site.

## Who hosts it?

The site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. Both process the
technical data any web request carries, such as your IP address, under their
own privacy policies.

## What about analytics?

Cloudflare's proxy adds its Web Analytics script to each page. The site's
content security policy only allows scripts from corrallib.com itself, so
browsers that enforce the policy do not load it.

## What happens to a message I send?

The contact form posts to Formspree, which delivers it by email. Your name,
email address and message are used only to reply to you.

## Does corralctl send data anywhere?

The corralctl binary talks to the forges you point it at, with your own
credentials. Its MCP server, in its default read-only mode, makes no network
calls at all.
